### Tips for Good Questions

1. **Be specific** — Use neuron names, VFB IDs, or FlyBase IDs
   - ✅ "DA1 lPN neurons"
   - ✅ "VFB_jrchjtdb"
   - ✅ "FBbt_00003748" (medulla)

2. **Ask for identifiers** — Always request VFB IDs for reproducibility
   - "…and give me the VFB IDs"
   - "…with their dataset names"

3. **Specify the dataset** when it matters
   - "in FlyWire"
   - "in the male-CNS"
   - "in BANC"

4. **One step at a time** — Chain related questions
   - First: "Find all X neurons"
   - Then: "Show me the top partners of [ID from above]"
   - Then: "What's the expression profile of [type from above]?"
